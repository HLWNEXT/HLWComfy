# Workflow: Clean Workspace & Temp Files

This workflow cleans up residual files generated by ComfyUI or crashed operations to free up space and ensure a fresh state.

1. Clean the ComfyUI `temp/` folder:
   `Remove-Item -Path "temp\*" -Recurse -Force -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue`
2. Clear standard Python cache (`__pycache__` folders):
   `Get-ChildItem -Path . -Filter "__pycache__" -Recurse -Directory | Remove-Item -Recurse -Force`
3. Optional: Clear old crash logs if they exceed a certain size or are older than 7 days.
4. Verify the directories are cleared by listing their contents.
